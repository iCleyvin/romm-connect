RoMM Connect - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Summary

RoMM Connect ("the App") is a free, open-source, unofficial mobile client for self-hosted RoMM servers. The App has no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your library, saves and account live on your RoMM server. The only information that reaches the developer is the technical data of the update check described in section 4.

2. What the App stores on your device

  • Server address of your RoMM server.
  • Session tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens, or the API token you provide), kept in the system's encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Your RoMM profile as returned by your server (username, role and e-mail address, if your account has one), cached so the App can open without a network round trip.
  • Theme preference.
  • Caches: cover images, and the game files and emulator settings that the built-in emulator keeps so a game starts faster next time.

Your password is never stored. It is sent once to your server to sign in and then discarded. Versions up to 0.5.0 kept it in encrypted storage; updating removes it.

App data is excluded from device backups.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used only while you scan a pairing QR code. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or transmitted.
  • Files: the App reads only the files you pick to upload, and writes only to the folder you choose when you download a ROM.
  • Network: required to reach your server.

5. What the developer does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your RoMM server, your credentials or your library.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

6. Security

Session tokens are stored in platform-encrypted storage. The App allows plain HTTP connections because many self-hosted servers run on a private network without a certificate. Over HTTP, your password and token travel unencrypted and can be read by anyone on the same network, so the App shows a warning before you sign in. Use HTTPS whenever your server is reachable from outside your home network.

7. Deleting your data

  • Sign out (Settings) removes the session tokens and cached profile.
  • Change server (Settings) also removes the server address.
  • Uninstalling the App removes everything it stored on the device.

Data on your RoMM server (saves, states, play history, API tokens) is managed from RoMM itself. To revoke an API token used by the App, delete it in your RoMM profile.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
